❓ Frequently Asked Questions

How many stadiums at the 2026 World Cup?

16 stadiums in 16 host cities: 11 in the USA, 2 in Canada, 3 in Mexico.

Where is the final played?

The final on July 19, 2026 is at MetLife Stadium in New Jersey.

Where is the opening match?

The opening match on June 11, 2026 (Mexico vs South Africa) takes place at Estadio Azteca in Mexico City.

What is the largest stadium?

The Estadio Azteca in Mexico with 87,000 seats. Followed by MetLife (82,500) and AT&T Stadium (80,000).
