❓ Frequently Asked Questions

When does the 2026 World Cup start?

The 2026 World Cup kicks off on Thursday, June 11, 2026 with the opening match Mexico vs South Africa at Estadio Azteca in Mexico City.

How many matches are there?

A total of 104 matches are scheduled, up from 64 in previous editions. This is the result of the expansion to 48 teams (instead of 32).

Where are the matches played?

The matches are spread across 16 stadiums in 3 host countries: the United States (78 matches), Canada (13 matches) and Mexico (13 matches).

How long does the tournament last?

The 2026 World Cup spans 39 days, from June 11 to July 19, 2026 — the longest World Cup in history.

Where is the final played?

The final on July 19, 2026 takes place at MetLife Stadium in New Jersey (New York metro area), capacity 82,500.
