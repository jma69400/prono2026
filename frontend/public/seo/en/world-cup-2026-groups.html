The 12 Official Groups of the 2026 World Cup

The official draw took place on December 5, 2025 in Washington, DC. The 48 qualified teams were distributed into 12 groups (A to L) of 4 teams each. England is in Group L with Croatia, Ghana, Panama.

🎯 How does the 12-group format work?

Each team plays 3 matches in its group. The top 2 from each group automatically qualify for the knockout stage, plus the 8 best third-placed teams. In total, 32 teams reach the Round of 32, a novelty of the 48-team format.
